# Code of Conduct

AI Fabric Framework contributors are expected to keep discussion respectful, practical, and focused on the work.

Do:

- assume good intent
- be specific and constructive
- respect maintainers' time
- keep security-sensitive details private

Do not:

- harass or attack contributors
- publish private credentials or customer data
- derail technical discussions with unrelated commercial or personal disputes

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
